Baked Lemon Pepper Salmon

Home

Recipe Description

Delicious Baked Lemon Pepper Salmon is an easy meal to prepare and goes great with a vegtable or grain side. All you need is a salmon filet, butter or oil, lemon pepper seasoning, and if you're like me, a drizzle of balsamic vinegar glaze.

Ingredients

  • salmon filet
  • lemon
  • black pepper
  • herbs
  • olive oil

Instructions

  1. Put salmon on a foil sheet and drizzle olive oil.
  2. Then season and top with lemon slices and herbs.
  3. Fold the foil around the salmon and preheat the oven to 375 degrees.
  4. Bake salmon for 18-20 minutes.
